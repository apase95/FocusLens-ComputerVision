# HierText attribution

Source: https://github.com/google-research-datasets/hiertext/tree/70b6620b2b112597d8219e11eee9773a1403827c

HierText is released under **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0**:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ . Retain the official LICENSE
and README cached under the subset's `source/` directory. Images originate
from Open Images, hosted by CVDF; each downloaded original's exact URL and
content hash are retained. Check original-image attribution/terms before any
redistribution; this notice does not independently establish every image's rights.

Derived line crops are bounding-box extractions, without OCR, text correction,
image enhancement or synthetic replacement. Preserve source attribution and
applicable share-alike obligations when distributing derivatives. Local assets
are ignored and are not included in the project's MIT source-code license.
The finalized 200 DEV / 500 TEST subset and convenience JSON exports preserve
raw labels, source identities and model visual-review evidence; they do not
constitute a human audit or grant additional redistribution rights.

Please cite: Shangbang Long, Siyang Qin, Dmitry Panteleev, Alessandro Bissacco,
Yasuhisa Fujii, Michalis Raptis. *Towards End-to-End Unified Scene Text Detection
and Layout Analysis*. CVPR 2022.

Also: Long et al. *ICDAR 2023 Competition on Hierarchical Text Detection and
Recognition*. arXiv:2305.09750, 2023.
